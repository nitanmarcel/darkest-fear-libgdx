# Changelog

## 1.1

- Fixed sounds playing over each other on desktop
- Turning sound on in Settings now starts the music, as in the original game
- Fixed the Rovio logo and title picture not showing at start-up
- Fixed the title menu flashing for a moment when continuing a paused game
- Fullscreen is now remembered between launches

## 1.0

First release.

- Runs the original Darkest Fear v1.55 (240x320) from your own JAR, chosen on first launch
- Desktop builds for Linux, Windows and macOS, and an Android APK
- Keyboard with rebindable keys, gamepad and touch controls
- Wide layout on desktop and in phone landscape, with an expanded view of the level and three zoom steps
- Enhanced lighting with coloured light and soft shadows
- Scale2x upscaling
- Cues for doors, closets and unlit lamps, and a warning before the dark hurts
- New menus, settings and in-game HUD
- Original lighting, view and pixels available in Settings
